## 2026-05-03 - [Fixing 404 Resource Paths and Eliminating Resource Discovery Delay for LCP]
**Learning:** In a static site with no build tool pipeline, hardcoded asset paths containing spaces (like background videos and favicon assets) or referring to non-existent directories (like `video/`) result in blockages and 404 resource errors, significantly impacting Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). Additionally, CDN scripts loaded via client-side template scripts suffer from a discovery delay. Resource preloading and preconnecting is the most efficient, lightweight optimization to address these bottlenecks without altering the site architecture.
**Action:** Always preconnect to critical CDNs (using `crossorigin`) and preload critical above-the-fold assets (like intro background videos and hero LCP images) in the HTML `<head>`. Ensure all local asset paths are URI-encoded and correctly referenced relative to the standard static folders (such as `assets/Batallando/`).
