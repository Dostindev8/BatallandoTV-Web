## 2026-05-24 - LCP Optimization and Asset Path Robustness
**Learning:** In this static repository, Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) was significantly delayed by three factors: late discovery of the hero image/video, lack of resource prioritization, and broken paths caused by unencoded spaces in filenames. Applying `fetchpriority="high"` and `<link rel="preload">` is critical for the hero assets, but must be paired with URI-encoding (e.g., `%20`) to ensure the browser can actually resolve the resources. Additionally, `decoding="async"` should be avoided on LCP images to prevent further rendering delays.
**Action:** Always URI-encode asset paths containing spaces and use a combination of `preload` and `fetchpriority` for above-the-fold content. Profile with a local server and Playwright to catch 404s early.
