## 2026-05-03 - Fixing Broken Paths and Optimizing LCP Rendering
**Learning:** Resolving 404 asset paths and unencoded spaces is a critical optimization step in standard HTML/JS applications. Unencoded spaces in asset paths cause connection delays or failures across environments, and pointing video assets to non-existent folders causes useless round-trip 404 network requests that delay critical Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). Selective head preloading of the primary brand logo and removing `decoding="async"` from eager LCP images with `fetchpriority="high"` significantly accelerates initial visually complete time. Additionally, using optional chaining and null-guards on DOM elements prevented runtime JS execution crashes.
**Action:** Always verify all media and asset paths recursively for spaces and encoding, pre-emptively load main above-the-fold assets, and ensure JS interactions with any conditional DOM elements are safely guarded.
