## 2026-03-01 - Optimizing LCP Asset Discovery and Connection Latency
**Learning:** In a static site architecture where key media assets are rendered dynamically or hidden beneath clientside JavaScript/CSS layouts (such as client-side templates and 3D overlays), the browser suffers from delayed resource discovery. Standardizing LCP asset paths, encoding dynamic asset spaces, utilizing preconnect resource hints withCrossOrigin attributes, and preloading core above-the-fold assets (the main hero image and background intro video) bypasses discovery delay. Additionally, fixing broken video paths (e.g. `images/SIN_FIN_-_BATALLANDO.mp4` and `video/batallando.mp4`) to the correct `assets/Batallando/` directory prevents expensive resource-scheduling stalls and 404 connection overhead.
**Action:** Always preload LCP images/videos in index.html head, resolve internal paths to proper URI-encoded locations, preconnect critical CDNs with crossorigin attributes, and avoid non-standard attributes on elements (like fetchpriority on links or video elements).
