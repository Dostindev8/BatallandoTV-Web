## 2025-05-15 - Redundant Video Preloading and Broken Asset Paths
**Learning:** In static sites with multiple media-rich components (like news cards), using `preload="auto"` or even `preload="metadata"` on multiple elements referencing the same large video file can lead to massive redundant bandwidth consumption (e.g., 26MB per card). Additionally, broken media paths (404s) on critical background assets like intro videos significantly degrade LCP and user experience.
**Action:** Always audit video preloading strategies. Use `preload="none"` for decorative or background videos in repeating elements, and reserve `preload="metadata"` for primary content. Ensure asset paths are relative and correctly URI-encoded, especially in environments without build-time asset processing.
