## 2026-05-03 - Fixing Hidden Performance Debt and LCP Bottlenecks
**Learning:** Broken asset paths (404s) for high-impact media like the intro video and favicon cause unnecessary network overhead and delay the critical rendering path. Additionally, the intro video (30MB+) was defaulting to `preload="metadata"`, but explicit optimization is needed to prevent it from competing with LCP assets. The preloader logo and the hero image ('assets/Batallando/Batallando6.png') are the primary LCP candidates, and their discovery can be accelerated using resource hints and fetch prioritization.
**Action:** Always audit the network tab for 404s at the start of a task. Apply `fetchpriority="high"` and `loading="eager"` to the earliest visible images. For large background videos, ensure `preload="metadata"` is set to prioritize initial HTML/CSS/JS delivery. In dynamic JS rendering, manually inject `loading="lazy"` into template literals for below-the-fold content.
