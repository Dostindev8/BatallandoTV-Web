## 2026-07-19 - Fixing 404 Video Assets and Implementing LCP Resource Hints
**Learning:** Autoplay video backgrounds pointing to incorrect paths (such as `images/...` instead of `assets/Batallando/...`) create hidden 404 errors that stall background layouts and waste network round-trips. Furthermore, client-side dynamic template literals delay critical above-the-fold asset discovery (like logos and hero images), deferring the Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) until after JavaScript execution.
**Action:** Always identify above-the-fold assets and preload them in the HTML head to bypass JS execution discovery delays. Restrict background videos to `preload="metadata"` and non-critical videos to `preload="none"` to save ~2.4MB of initial payload bandwidth, and ensure all asset paths are robustly URI-encoded without leading slashes for relative URL consistency.
